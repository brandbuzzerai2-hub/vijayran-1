A palace for the romantic, the celebrant, and those looking for the extraordinary.

The Vijayran Palace – our heritage resort in Jaipur, Rajasthan is one of the finest royal resorts in entire Rajasthan. Set in the lap of the ancient Aravalli hills near Amer, you set your soul free and let your mind wander into the pristine, serene and solitary surroundings that sing the poetry of nature. An oasis of luxury and regal ambience that offers the perfect blend of modern age luxuries and a spiritual getaway.

Weddings At The Vijayran Palace

What can be more beautiful than a regal style wedding where the prince charming swoops his princess off feet. Royal tapestry, artistic décor and exotic ambience! The Vijayran Palace gifts you the amazing palace grandeur where you can choose to have a wedding like the Kings and Queens of yester-years. Rustic authentic feast, exotic backdrops for pre and post wedding photo shoots, beautiful theme-based décor and musical nights.

Photo Shoots

Pre - Weddings

The Vijayran Palace’s beautiful architecture, lush greenery, Jharokas, the rugged Mountain View in backdrop and sunset by the pool side are an added attraction for the groom and bride who awaits their photo shoots with great enthusiasm. Imagine the photo shoots where you could be the royal queen standing in a Jharoka and your prince charming with his enigmatic smile stealing glances right beside you.

Celebrations

Meeting & Event

The Vijayran Palace offers another marvel in form of an on demand cultural show exclusively for wedding guests that include Rajasthan’s folk songs, folk dances and the much appreciated Ghoomar. Let the wedding guests twirl, sing & dance, enjoy their heart’s content! A taste of Royal Rajasthan’s colorful culture and vibrancy. Let the music flow and feet tap in sync with the festive mood.
